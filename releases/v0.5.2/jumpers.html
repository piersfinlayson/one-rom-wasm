One ROM board header pin viewer

A generic view of a board's physical jumper / programming header from the live board_info(board).jumper_header metadata. Each pad is drawn in its physical position (pin 1 top-left, odd pins top row, even pins bottom row; X pads on the lower row where present), labelled with its role(s). Boards not yet characterised show nothing.
